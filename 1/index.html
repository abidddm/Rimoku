Heritage

From Al Madinah

Al Madinah has been known for its date palms for centuries. Rimoku brings a focused range of its finest varieties, from the honeyed Sukkari to the prized Ajwa, to UK tables.

Every variety is sold as whole dates with nothing added: ingredients are simply dates (100%). Each pack carries its best before date and lot stamp, with full product information in English and Arabic.
